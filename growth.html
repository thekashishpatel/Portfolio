Liquor delivery app

Cheers By United

About the brand

Cheers By United is a liquor delivery app that home-delivers liquor within 30 minutes.

Challenge

The app was in its introduction stage. It lacked brand awareness and app installs.

Solution

Created an effective social media strategy and digital ad campaigns with on-point communication.

Approach in market

Ran two parallel campaigns: a Mumbai-wide awareness push built on the "30 mins" delivery promise, and a dedicated app-install campaign to convert that awareness into downloads.

Result

Built brand awareness with 7,50,000+ audience reached at ₹0.19 cost per result, and drove 4,000+ app installs at ₹10 cost per install.

D2C clothing brand

Suusst Panda

About the brand

A clothing brand designing t-shirts, hoodies, and customizable jackets for panda lovers.

Challenge

The brand was unable to generate online sales, despite already running heavy influencer marketing.

Solution

Built a smoother consumer journey from ad to checkout, and launched digital ads with offers designed to convert.

Approach in market

Split the budget between a PAN-India awareness carousel and a dedicated conversion campaign built for website purchases, rather than relying on influencer reach alone.

Result

Generated 30+ conversions at ₹632.91 cost per purchase, and reached 2,75,000+ audience across the campaign.
