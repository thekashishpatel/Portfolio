Video

How I run it

I can take a video the full distance: concept and script, directing the shoot, including coaching nervous first-timers through being on camera, editing the footage myself, and posting it. Every video starts as a creative idea for communicating what the brand actually offers; the shot list and edit just follow from that.

Where others come in

When a project needs more hands or a specific skill I don't run myself, I brief and direct freelance editors, videographers, or a production team, and hold the finished piece to the same standard I'd hit doing it solo.
