Real estate developer

Shree Balaji Group

About the project

Shree Balaji Agora City Centre is a "city within a city" development in Vadodara: 72 2BHK, 168 3BHK, 99 4BHK, 21 penthouses, and 190 offices across six towers, with a 2.10-lakh sq ft podium club and 40+ amenities.

Goal

Build strong brand awareness for a newly launched project and generate 300+ qualified leads.

Target audience

Business owners, directors, and CEOs aged 27–65 across Vadodara and neighboring cities, profiled as investment-minded, luxury-lifestyle buyers with an interest in high-value property.

Strategy

A four-stage funnel: Meta brand-awareness ads for reach, video ads to build interest, lead-gen ads with retargeting to drive decisions, and a site-visit push to close, phased over three campaign cycles and piped straight into the sales team's CRM.

Design education platform

Step Zero Academy

About the brand

A multilingual online platform simplifying design education through community-led, cohort-based courses that help graphic designers become problem-solving brand designers who can command high-ticket clients.

Goal

Generate ₹5,00,000 in revenue within 30–45 days by moving traffic up a value ladder toward the ₹15,000 mentorship program.

Target audience

Graphic designers, freelancers, and creative-agency owners aged 18–30 studying or working in design, concentrated in Mumbai, Pune, Bangalore, Hyderabad, and other South Indian markets.

Strategy

Three parallel funnels, a free workshop, a ₹499 paid workshop, and a ₹99 recorded webinar, each engineered to upsell into the ₹15,000 mentorship program, backed by brand-driven, product-driven, urgency, and coach-credibility content across the ad set.

Traction so far

Two months in: ~₹70,000 invested across 2 live workshops generated ₹53,483 in revenue, 3 upsells into the ₹15,000 course plus 17 enrollments in the ₹499 workshop, with 700 customer records banked for custom and lookalike audiences.
